Privacy Policy for HotShot Espresso Finder

Last Updated: December 2025

Thank you for using HotShot Espresso Finder (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping your experience simple and enjoyable.

What We Do

HotShot Espresso Finder helps you find quality espresso cafes by showing a list and map of nearby cafes. You can search cafes and see your current location on the map to find places near you. We do not require you to create an account or sign in, and there is no paid content.

Information We Collect

  • Location Data: To show your location on the map and help you find nearby cafes, the app accesses your device’s location. Your location is processed only on your device and is not sent to or stored by us.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect any personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. We do not require you to create an account.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not use third-party trackers or analytics tools. We do not collect or share any data for advertising or marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • Your location is used solely to display your position on the map and improve your cafe search experience.
  • We do not store or transmit your location data to any servers.
  • All cafe information is publicly available and displayed without personal data collection.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, the risk of data breaches is minimal. Your location stays on your device, and we take standard measures to keep the app safe and secure.

Links to Other Websites

HotShot Espresso Finder may link to external websites for cafe details or additional information. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party websites. Please review their privacy policies separately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to keep it accurate. We encourage you to review it whenever you use the app. Significant changes will be communicated on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@hotshotespresso.app.
